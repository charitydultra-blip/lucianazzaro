Lucia Nazzaro is an Emmy-nominated storyteller, doctoral researcher, and licensed mental health expert exploring the intersection of human experience, culture, wellbeing, and emerging technology. But mostly, she is human.

Her work brings together storytelling, research, and real-life conversations to explore how we live, love, connect, change, and remain human in a rapidly evolving world.

Her background combines an undergraduate degree from the University of New Hampshire, a master’s degree in Media Arts from Emerson College, and a master’s degree from Boston University’s Graduate School of Clinical Social Work, with a focus on psychotherapy and social issues.

Her doctoral work at the University of Pennsylvania focuses on media, emerging technology, and wellbeing.
