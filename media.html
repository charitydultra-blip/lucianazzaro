01 / Create

Executive producer

Lucia is the creator and founder of WellSeekers and the host of WellSeekers with Lucia. The platform shares stories and tools focused on authentic living and wellbeing.

She also co-founded Betty Everything, where she co-created, co-hosted, executive produced, and wrote for a daily multimedia website for women. Her producing work spans streaming, broadcast, and digital media.

02 / Discover

Journalist & host

Lucia earned two Emmy nominations as a host of The CW Network weekday primetime with Tribune Broadcasting’s WCCT/WTIC.

Her career includes iHeartMedia’s WERZ and STAR, co-hosting and executive producing a nationally syndicated show at Bus Radio, and storytelling at Only Good TV.
