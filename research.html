Lucia’s doctoral work at the University of Pennsylvania brings together media, emerging technology, and wellbeing. Her interests connect academic inquiry with the stories and experiences of everyday life.

With training in media arts and clinical social work, she approaches questions about change through both storytelling and an understanding of mental and emotional health.

Experience in practice

From inquiry
to conversation.

Lucia’s training has included resilience, human potential, relationships, cognitive behavioral therapy, relational therapies, and narrative therapy.

Her background also includes education, outreach, and mental health advocacy. As a former National Director of Education and Outreach at Herren Project, she managed programs serving more than 600 school sites nationwide.

Across these experiences, her focus is on making information accessible and exploring stories and solutions that support individual and societal wellbeing.
